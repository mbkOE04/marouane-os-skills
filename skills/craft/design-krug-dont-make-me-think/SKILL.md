# Don't Make Me Think (Krug)
## When to use
Designing pages and navigation; running cheap usability tests.
## Principles
- Make each page self-evident; if not, self-explanatory.
- People scan, satisfice and muddle through; they do not read.
- Omit needless words. Make clickable things obvious.
- Test with three users monthly; fix the worst problems first.
## Checklist
- Can a new user say what the page is and what to do in 5 seconds?
- Is the primary action the most prominent element?
- Is the way back and the current location always visible?
## Questions
Where would a first-time user click, and why?
