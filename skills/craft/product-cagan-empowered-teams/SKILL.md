# Empowered product teams (Cagan, SVPG)
## When to use
Setting team goals, writing a roadmap, judging whether a team ships outputs or solves problems.
## Principles
- Give teams problems to solve, with outcome goals, not a list of features.
- Cross-functional team: product, design, engineering together from discovery.
- Discovery tests four risks: value, usability, feasibility, viability.
- Leaders supply context and strategy; teams own the solution.
## Checklist
- Is each roadmap item phrased as an outcome with a measure?
- Was value and feasibility tested before committing to build?
- Does the team have access to customers and data?
## Questions
What problem does this solve, for whom, and how will we know it worked?
