# The Design of Everyday Things (Norman)
## When to use
Designing interactions; explaining why users err; reviewing forms and controls.
## Principles
- Discoverability: affordances, signifiers, constraints, mapping.
- Feedback and a conceptual model users can form.
- Human error is a design failure; design for slips and mistakes.
- Two gulfs: execution (how do I do it) and evaluation (what happened).
## Checklist
- Is each control's use signalled?
- Does every action give immediate, visible feedback?
- Can errors be prevented or reversed?
## Questions
What did the user think would happen?
