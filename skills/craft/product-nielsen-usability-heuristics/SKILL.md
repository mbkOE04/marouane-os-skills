# Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics
## When to use
Reviewing a screen, flow or prototype before or after build; triaging usability bugs.
## Principles
1. Visibility of system status. 2. Match between system and real world. 3. User control and freedom. 4. Consistency and standards. 5. Error prevention. 6. Recognition over recall. 7. Flexibility and efficiency of use. 8. Aesthetic and minimalist design. 9. Help users recognise, diagnose and recover from errors. 10. Help and documentation.
## Checklist
- Does each step show where the user is and what happens next?
- Are labels in the user's words, not internal terms?
- Can the user undo or exit every step?
- Are destructive or costly actions guarded?
- Are error messages plain, specific and constructive?
## Questions to ask
Which heuristic does this violate, how severe (cosmetic to blocker), and how often will users hit it?
