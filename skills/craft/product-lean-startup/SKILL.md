# Lean Startup
## When to use
Early product bets, new features with uncertain demand, deciding persevere or pivot.
## Principles
- Build, Measure, Learn; minimise time through the loop.
- Validated learning over opinions. Smallest experiment (MVP) that tests the riskiest assumption.
- Innovation accounting: actionable metrics, not vanity metrics.
- Pivot or persevere on evidence.
## Checklist
- State the hypothesis and the metric that would disprove it.
- Define the MVP and a time box.
- Decide in advance what result triggers a pivot.
## Questions
What is the riskiest assumption? What is the cheapest test?
