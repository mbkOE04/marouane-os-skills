# Design sprint (Knapp, GV)
## When to use
A high-stakes question, a stuck team, or a need to test an idea before building.
## Principles
Five days: Map, Sketch, Decide, Prototype, Test. Small cross-functional team with a decider. Fake the front end, test with five users.
## Checklist
- Long-term goal and sprint questions written on day 1.
- One target moment chosen.
- Prototype only what the test needs.
## Questions
What would we need to believe for this to work?
