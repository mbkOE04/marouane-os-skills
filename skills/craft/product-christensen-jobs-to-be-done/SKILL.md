# Jobs To Be Done
## When to use
Understanding why customers "hire" a product; positioning; finding competitors beyond the category.
## Principles
- Customers buy progress in a circumstance, not a product.
- Competition is whatever else gets the job done, including doing nothing.
- Segment by job, not by demographics.
## Checklist
- Write the job: "When ___, I want to ___, so I can ___."
- Interview recent switchers about what triggered the change.
- List functional, emotional and social dimensions.
## Questions
What were they using before? What made them switch?
