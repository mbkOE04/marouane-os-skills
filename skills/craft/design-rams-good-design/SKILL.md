# Rams: ten principles of good design
## When to use
Design critique; cutting scope; deciding what to remove.
## Principles
Good design is innovative, makes a product useful, is aesthetic, makes a product understandable, is unobtrusive, honest, long-lasting, thorough down to the last detail, environmentally friendly, and as little design as possible.
## Checklist
- What can be removed without loss?
- Does the design make the product's function obvious?
- Does it promise nothing it cannot deliver?
## Questions
Is this needed, or decoration?
